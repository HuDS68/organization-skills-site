📝 Tips to Get Started

  • Create a daily to-do list and prioritize 3 key tasks
  • Use the Bullet Journal Method
  • Declutter your space regularly—mental space follows physical space
  • Try the Eisenhower Matrix to decide what's urgent vs important

🔧 Free Tools

  • Todoist – Task management with prioritization
  • Trello – Visual boards for organizing projects
  • Notion – All-in-one workspace for notes, projects, and habits
  • Google Calendar – Plan your days with reminders
